### Non-Blocking Findings

1. **LOG-02: Completed tasks excluded** — `allTasks` query filters `eq(tasks.completed, false)`. A user cannot add a manual log to an already-completed task. Not stated as a constraint in requirements — implicit scope decision made during implementation.

2. **LOG-03: Time-of-day not implemented** — Requirements say "start date and time" but only a date picker exists. Start time is stored as local noon (12:00) as a placeholder. `toLocalDateStr()` round-trip ensures the entry appears under the correct day in Reports. Functional for the stated use case; the "time" precision was dropped silently.
